Axiom 自己的四条公理Our four axioms

公理Axiom 1

数学就在日常里。Mathematics lives in everyday life.

校园生活和社会生活里，有很多有意思的现象。换成数学的眼光，常常能看出别的东西。Campus life and city life are full of curious things. Seen through mathematics, they often turn out to be something else.

公理Axiom 2

先弄清问题，再动手算。Understand the problem before you calculate.

建模的第一步，是从现实里提取条件、确立前提。公式在这之后才出场。Modeling begins by pulling conditions out of reality and fixing the premises. Formulas come later.

公理Axiom 3

对数学好奇，就够了。Curiosity is enough.

基础好不好，关系没有想象中那么大。在这里，每个对数学感兴趣的人都能找到自己的位置。Where you start matters less than you might think. Anyone who is interested in mathematics can find a place here.

公理Axiom 4

有趣和有用，可以兼得。Fun and useful can go together.

我们尽量让数学保持好玩，也教实用、高效的方法，给以后的学习打个底子。We keep mathematics playful and teach practical, efficient methods that will support your later studies.

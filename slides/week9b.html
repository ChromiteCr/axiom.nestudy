GH · 第一句

0:08–0:09

每一节的第一句写结论

不要写

Figure 3 shows the results of the sensitivity analysis.

写成

Check time drives the sweep time: from 19 s to 135 s, T rises from 66 s to 414 s, while walking speed moves T by about 11 s.

GH · 假设

0:10–0:11

每条假设写三样：内容、理由、影响

Assumption　Checking one room takes c = 60 s.
Justification　Within the 19–135 s range measured by Hsiao et al. (2016).
Impact　T grows linearly with c; see Section 6.

GH · 灵敏度一节

0:11–0:13

灵敏度一节放在结果之后，结论写范围和条件

Results→Sensitivity Analysis→Strengths and Weaknesses

不要写

The model is relatively sensitive to c.

写成

When c varies by ±10%, T changes by ±24 s and the assignment stays the same.

GH · 公式与引用

0:32–0:33

公式用 \eqref，文献用 \cite

\begin{equation}
  T = \max_k T_k \label{eq:T}
\end{equation}
By \eqref{eq:T}, the sweep ends when the last responder finishes.
The check time follows \cite{hsiao2016}.     % refs.bib 里的键名

\bibliographystyle{plain}
\bibliography{refs}

GH · 编译报错

0:33–0:35

编译报错先看第一条

报错常见原因
Undefined control sequence命令拼错，或忘了加宏包
Missing $ inserted正文里直接写了 _ 或 ^，没放进 $ $
File not found图的路径或文件名写错，大小写也算
Citation undefined键名不对，或 refs.bib 没接上
Overfull \hbox一行太长溢出页边，是警告，看 PDF 再决定改不改
